Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Effective: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Vidora: All Video Downloader mobile application (the "App", "we", "us") handles information, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By installing or using the App you agree to this Policy.

The App has no account or login and never asks for your social media credentials. Links you paste and the videos you download are processed on your device — we do not run servers that receive your links or your files.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Links — the video links you paste, share to the App, or allow it to read from your clipboard. They are used on your device to fetch the video directly from the platform that hosts it.
  • Downloaded media — videos, audio and images you choose to save. They are stored only on your device, in your gallery and/or in the App's private library.
  • Private Vault PIN — stored on your device only as a salted hash. We cannot see or recover it.
  • Preferences — settings such as theme, default quality and Quick Share, stored on your device.
  • Purchase records — if purchases are offered, subscription status is managed by Google Play Billing. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Support messages — anything you send to innovatroid@gmail.com.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • App installation ID — a random identifier from Firebase Installations, used to load remote app configuration and to enforce abuse blocks.
  • Device & app data — device model, Android version, app version and language, as collected by our service providers below.
  • Advertising data — the Android Advertising ID and ad interaction data (ads shown or clicked), collected by Google AdMob to serve, measure and frequency-cap ads.

1.3 Clipboard

When "Magic clipboard" is on, the App checks your clipboard when you open it, only to offer a copied video link. The clipboard contents never leave your device. You can turn this off in Settings.

1.4 Permissions we request

  • Internet & Network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to download videos, update the download engine, load remote configuration and show ads.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show download progress and completion.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC) — so downloads keep running while the App is in the background.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by AdMob. You can reset or delete it in your device's Google settings > Ads.

Saving to your gallery uses Android's MediaStore, which does not need a storage permission. The App does not access your other photos or files.

2. How We Use Information

  • To fetch and save the videos you request.
  • To keep the download engine working by checking for engine updates.
  • To display ads — banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads — and measure their performance.
  • To process purchases and confirm entitlement, where offered.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, and to comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

3. Third-Party Services

The following services receive data when you use the App. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database for app configuration, Installations) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing & In-App Review — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Video platforms — when you download a link, your device connects directly to the site hosting it (for example Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Reddit, Vimeo), which may log your IP address under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub — engine updates are downloaded from GitHub — GitHub Privacy Statement

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising

The free App is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and unlock a feature in exchange for watching. Where required by law, you will be shown a consent choice for personalised or non-personalised ads, which you can change later in your device's Google settings > Ads.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Downloads, library, vault and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall it.
  • Installation ID is reset when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Data held by Google services (AdMob, Firebase, Play) is kept according to Google's retention policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, India under the DPDP Act 2023, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Email innovatroid@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Connections use TLS where the platform supports it, and your vault PIN is stored only as a salted hash. No system is perfectly secure; contact us if you have a concern.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be highlighted in the App.

10. Contact
